The differences matter

Two sites exposed explicit reCAPTCHA key-configuration errors before any inquiry was entered. Vanuatu exposed a database-login error; Cape Town exposed a mail SSL-protocol error. Sarajevo and Sri Lanka displayed conflicting sent and failed notices. Other forms simply cleared or kept loading. These observations identify different failure stages; they do not identify one common actor.

The BioInnovation Institute failure included an assistant preparation mistake: a 66-character subject exceeded a 50-character field limit. After correction, the user received an acknowledgment. This is retained alongside provider-side and unresolved observations.

Some image challenges could not be operated through the browser-control tool. That limitation is not evidence that the CAPTCHA service failed. No controlled comparison with an ordinary browser, another network or alternate message content was performed.
